        How does this work?
        

          Your audio is converted to a 128×128 RGB Mel-spectrogram and fed to a
          convolutional neural network (CNN) trained on labelled soundscape data.
          The network outputs a probability that the recording is
          natural (birdsong, wind, water, etc.) or
          anthropogenic (traffic, machinery, voices, etc.).
